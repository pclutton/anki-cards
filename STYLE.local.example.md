# My Style Tweaks

Copy this file to `STYLE.local.md` and edit it. That copy is ignored by git, so
your changes are never overwritten by an update and never cause a conflict when
you `git pull`.

You do **not** need to repeat everything from `STYLE.md` — only what you want
different. Anything in here beats the shared guide where the two disagree.

Delete the examples below and write your own.

---

## Card count

Make 15–20 cards per lecture rather than 20–30 — I'd rather do fewer and
actually keep up with them.

## Topics to skip

- Anything about lab equipment or protocols; not examined on my course
- The historical/discovery sections

## Emphasis

- Always tag cardiovascular material `cardio` so I can filter it
- My exam is short-answer, not multiple choice — phrase questions to require a
  written answer rather than a recognition

## My own worked example

Examples shape the output more than rules do, so one from your own subject is
the single most useful thing you can add here.

**Bad** — too vague to mark honestly:

> **Q:** Describe the immune response.
> **A:** Innate and adaptive systems respond to pathogens.

**Good** — specific enough to grade:

> **Q:** What distinguishes the adaptive immune response from the innate one?
> **A:** It is specific to a particular pathogen and it forms memory — a second
> exposure produces a faster, stronger response.
